About Us

Our Story

Crochify by Fari started with a simple love for yarn and a crochet hook. What began as a hobby, slowly grew into a small handmade brand, where every piece is made stitch by stitch with patience, care and a lot of love.

What We Make

From hair accessories, gajray, bouquets, keychains, bags, phone covers, jewellery, hijab pins, Quran covers, baby wear and soft toys, everything in our shop is crocheted by hand. No two pieces are exactly alike, and that is what makes them special.

Why Handmade?

Handmade means time, attention and a personal touch. We choose soft, good-quality yarn, finish every piece neatly, and pack your order with care, so it feels like a real gift, whether it is for you or for someone you love.

Custom Orders

Have a colour, a design or a gift idea in mind? We love making custom pieces. Message us on WhatsApp with your idea and we will tell you the price and delivery time.

How to Order

  1. Choose your favourite product from our shop.
  2. Tap "Order on WhatsApp". Your message is ready, just press send.
  3. We confirm the details, price and delivery with you.
  4. Your order is packed with love and sent to you.

Thank you for supporting a small handmade business. Every order truly makes our day.
